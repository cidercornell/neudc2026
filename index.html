Overview

The North East Universities Development Consortium (NEUDC) 2026 will be held in person at Cornell University in Ithaca, NY on November 7 and 8, 2026. The conference is organized by the Collaboration for International Development Economics Research (CIDER), a cross-campus initiative at Cornell.

NEUDC has organized annual conferences on development economics since 1967, making it the longest-running gathering in the field. The conference rotates annually among its organizing institutions: Boston University, Brown University, Cornell University, Dartmouth College, Harvard University, MIT, Northeastern University, Tufts University, and Yale University.

Call for Papers

We are now accepting submissions. All are welcome to apply, with special consideration given to work by graduate students and early-stage researchers.

By submitting a paper to this event, authors acknowledge that accepted submissions will be made publicly available as part of the conference proceedings and/or on the event website.

Before you submit

Please have the following information ready:

  • Presenter name, title, and institutional affiliation
  • Year PhD was received (or expected)
  • Whether this paper is your job market paper (JMP)
  • Author composition (e.g., faculty only, students only, or a mix)
  • The fields most relevant to your paper (used for reviewer assignment)

Register

Registration opens July 1, 2026.

Full registration is $300; the reduced rate is $200 for those who qualify (see below).

Rate Applies to
$300 Faculty and professionals at universities and research institutions.
$200 Eligible if you meet one of the criteria in the list below.

Who qualifies for the reduced rate?

  • PhD students
  • Post-docs
  • Researchers based in a low or middle-income country
  • Faculty at community colleges
  • Faculty at historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs)

For a U.S. visa invitation letter, email the conference office.

Included with your fee

  • Breakfast and lunch on both full program days
  • Dinner on the first conference evening
  • Coffee breaks
